### The project:
During my time at Triangle Factory, I started working as a developer in a B2B scenario, where we developed VR Trainings for Transfr.

**Created with** Unity3D, Unity Meta Package, In-house framework

**Developed for** Meta Quest 2


### My role:
My day-to-day work consisted of the following:
- Making time estimates.
- Communicating with the designer about issues or possible tweaks we came across.
- Analyzing and maintaining the in-house framework.
- Going back to older products and bringing them up to par with the new quality standard. 

Some examples of the shipped modules for [Transfr](https://transfrinc.com/) I worked on:
* [Electrical construction](https://transfrinc.com/products/electrical-construction/).
* [Diesel training](https://transfrinc.com/products/diesel/).
* [Aviation](https://transfrinc.com/products/aviation/).
